SmartTraffic AI · Squad Nova4 / 7
Member 2 — 1 min Our solution comes down to three steps. 1. We collect GPS traces from the phones of a partner fleet's couriers — they're already riding all day. We snap each point to the right street. 2. A model learns two things, segment by segment: are motorbikes getting through right now, and how fast. If no motorbike goes down a street anymore right after a downpour, that's a strong signal. 3. The fleet manager sees this on a dashboard: a realistic delivery time, with a margin of error, and the streets to avoid. Stress the bottom line: the exact task of the AI. The time of a delivery is the sum of the predicted times on each segment of the route. Transition (hand over): “Our teammate will show you where it gets hard.”

Member 3 — 50 s Three challenges, and this is where the real AI work is. 1. GPS: couriers have entry-level phones. The position jumps, sometimes from one street to the next. We snap each trace to the most likely path in the street network — this is called map-matching. 2. Volume: main roads get lots of traffic, some alleys very little. The model treats the city as a graph: a rarely used alley learns from its neighbours and from streets that look like it. 3. Rain: a street that's passable in the morning can be flooded in the afternoon. We don't settle for an average: we detect live the streets motorbikes start avoiding, and cross-check with measured rainfall. Transition: “To get there, we chose a very tight scope.”
